Programs & Content

Programs and contents

Training and consulting programs designed for companies, managers and sales teams operating in international contexts or preparing to enter new markets.

Core program

Cross-Cultural Negotiation

A practical path to prepare and conduct international negotiations with stronger method, cultural awareness and trust-building capability.

  • Negotiation preparation and objective setting.
  • Reading the cultural context and counterpart’s negotiation style.
  • Managing objections, timing, silence, concessions and follow-up.
  • Role play, simulations and debriefing on real cases.

Negotiation Training

Training for sales teams, business developers and managers who deal with complex clients, partners and stakeholders.

International Business Communication

Professional communication in international contexts: email, meetings, presentations, commercial relationships and cultural differences.

Export & Market Entry

Support for companies preparing a new-market entry strategy, focusing on positioning, channels, partners and commercial actions.

Sales, Marketing & Operations

Content on commercial strategy, pricing, P/L, forecasting, channels, Incoterms, logistics and export procedures.

Online & In-company Programs

Workshops, corporate training, executive coaching, role play and tailored programs delivered in person or online.

Content structure

Possible modules

01Negotiation strategy

Objectives, margins, alternatives, stakeholders, decision map and negotiation-table preparation.

02Culture and relationship

Trust building, communication differences, context reading and style adaptation.

03Simulations and role play

Practical cases, guided exercises, objection handling and operational feedback.

04Export readiness

Market analysis, positioning, commercial channels, local partners and action plan.

05International go-to-market

Pricing, forecasts, P/L, commercial communication and development of concrete opportunities.

06Follow-up and performance

Post-meeting actions, relationship management, measurement of results and consolidation of contacts.
