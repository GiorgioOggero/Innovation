Not theoretical training. Real experience transformed into method.

Negotiation training is often full of theory.

But companies do not need another abstract course. They need practical tools to prepare negotiations, understand the context, avoid cultural mistakes, handle objections, read the customer and protect the value of their offer.

After years of experience in export, international sales, market development and corporate training, I have decided to focus my work on one clear objective: bringing into the classroom what is usually learned only in the field.

Not theoretical training. Real business experience turned into method.

02

How many deals have you lost not on price?

How many deals have you lost not on price, but because you didn’t truly understand who you were negotiating with?

In international business, negotiation is rarely just about numbers. It is about context, culture, trust, timing, decision-making dynamics and the way value is presented, defended and perceived.

Many companies approach international markets with strong products, but without a clear method to prepare, manage and close complex conversations across cultures.

Real experience. Practical method. Applicable results.
