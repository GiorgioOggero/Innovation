Behind the title

A different way of looking at the world.

A squirrel, an acorn and a crocodile: an unexpected question opens the door to a wider reflection on the way we communicate, persuade and understand one another.

The Charm of Doubt explores the value of questioning what seems obvious. Through the lens of communication and human relationships, it invites readers to consider fresh perspectives and more conscious choices.
